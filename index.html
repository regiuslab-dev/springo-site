Privacy Policy — Springo

Application: Springo

Package name: com.regiuslab.springo

Developer / Publisher: Siarhei Piachko

Last updated: October 2026. Springo is a casual endless jumper game. Privacy questions: regius.lab@gmail.com.

1. Data we collect (on your device)

This app stores data locally on your device using local storage (for example SharedPreferences or similar on-device storage). The app does not require account registration. We do not operate our own backend servers to collect or store your game progress or app settings.

Depending on how you use the app, local data may include:

  • Player profiles — display names and best scores you create on the device;
  • Gameplay preferences — such as control scheme (on-screen / tilt), selected character silhouette, and selected world theme pack;
  • Sound preferences — whether sound effects and theme ambience are enabled;
  • Content trials — temporary unlocks of optional themes or characters obtained after watching a rewarded ad (expiry stored on device);
  • Disclaimer acceptance — whether you have acknowledged the first-launch privacy notice;
  • Ad frequency helpers — local timestamps or counters used only to space automatic ads reasonably (for example install grace / play-time gaps), not a cloud profile.

Gameplay itself (platforms, score during a run) runs on your device. We do not upload your scores or settings to our own servers.

We do not ask for your contacts, precise location, photos, microphone access, or other sensitive device data for the app’s own features. Optional sensors (for example accelerometer for tilt controls) are used only for in-game movement when you choose that control mode.

Yandex and its advertising partners act as independent processors and handle data under Yandex’s policies and your device settings.

For ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and related purposes, such services may process categories of information that typically include:

  • Advertising/device identifiers, such as the Google advertising ID (GAID) or similar identifiers on Android, where available;
  • App-related metadata, such as the application package name and version;
  • Device and software information, such as device model, display parameters, operating system version, language, and country/region;
  • Network-related information, such as network connection type, carrier identifiers where available, and IP address;
  • Approximate location derived from technical signals (for example IP-based region) where used for advertising;
  • Ad interaction data, such as impressions, clicks, reward completions, or related events.

Google Play builds do not include Huawei Ads Kit. For details on how Yandex processes data for advertising, see Yandex’s published materials, including the Yandex Ads privacy policy and Yandex Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure guidance. We do not operate Yandex’s advertising systems.

You may be able to limit ad personalization or reset your advertising identifier in your Android device settings (Google / Ads settings), where available.
